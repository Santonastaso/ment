# Rights requests, export, withdrawal and deletion — draft

Rights-request owner, privacy contact, authorized identity verifier, escalation deputy and request-system location: **PENDING named assignment**. Applicable response deadlines, exceptions and record-retention policy: **PENDING controller/qualified review**. Do not imply these procedures are staffed or legally approved.

## Request handling

1. Receive through the approved private channel once established; create a restricted case with received time, scope and opaque ID. Avoid collecting an identity document by default. Verify identity proportionately through authenticated account or another approved route, including authority for representatives.
2. Determine the requested right and scope: access/export, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, optional CV withdrawal or future AI opt-out. Separate identity confirmation, legal decision and technical fulfillment. Record deadline calculation and extensions/refusal rationale through the assigned privacy lead; no deadline is invented in this draft.
3. Locate account, imported source records, messages, discovery, reflections, consents, calendar, notifications, logs, backups and provider copies. Review other people's rights before releasing shared records. Use least-privilege operators; do not paste lookup results into GitHub.
4. Fulfill or explain partial fulfillment through the authenticated/private delivery route. Validate permissions and scope with synthetic test accounts first. Confirm completion and residual limits; retain only minimal case evidence under the approved schedule.

## Backups, third parties and partial failure

Active database/storage deletion is not proof that all copies are erased. Supabase backup/PITR retention and Storage backup coverage, Vercel/Supabase/Resend logs, Mistral request retention, exports held by members, delivered emails and Google/Microsoft events have separate lifecycles. Their durations and deletion capabilities are **UNKNOWN/PENDING verification**. Do not promise immediate, universal or irreversible deletion.

Account erasure removes local calendar links/tokens; that is not evidence of remote event cancellation or OAuth grant revocation. Coordinate external cleanup before losing the necessary credentials where appropriate, using an approved operator/provider process. Other participants may retain messages or event invitations. Explain lawful retention/exceptions and provider limitations after controller review, without promising recall of recipients' copies.

Draft recovery rule: maintain a restricted minimal erasure/suppression ledger with request completion time and necessary identifiers under an approved basis/schedule. That ledger and recovery automation are **not established by this repo**. On restore, isolate the recovered system, reapply completed erasure/restriction decisions, disable outbound email/calendar jobs, validate synthetic deletion and only then authorize access. Set and test backup expiry, RPO/RTO and purge-on-restore procedure. If cleanup or login closure is pending, report partial failure, assign retry and follow through; do not close the request as fully erased based solely on a 200 response or CI pass.
