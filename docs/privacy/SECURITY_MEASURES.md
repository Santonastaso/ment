Draft operating cadence: named custodian reviews least-privilege access at onboarding, offboarding and a controller-approved periodic interval; maintains dependency remediation and credential rotation records; checks retention and release failures; exercises recovery and incident handling. Interval, on-call coverage and all named owners remain pending. Do not record completed reviews without evidence.

Application audit/AI records may include stable member identifiers even when content-free. Other Edge logs can include IDs or provider error details. Do not claim all application logs are sanitized. Restrict access, assess payload risks, select retention and establish a protected incident evidence route before production acceptance. CI sanitization introduced here covers its outcome artifacts and production lint/RLS output; it does not redact historical logs or all release command output.
